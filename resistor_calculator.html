R Resistor Calculator

Series & parallel combinations · by Duncan Johnson

Need a non-standard resistor value? Two standard resistors combined in series or parallel can often land closer to your target than any single off-the-shelf part. Series combinations are easy to work out by hand — parallel ones, less so. This tool searches the E6 (20%), E12 (10%), E24 (5%), E48 (2%) and E96 (1%) series and ranks every combination that beats the nearest single standard value.
